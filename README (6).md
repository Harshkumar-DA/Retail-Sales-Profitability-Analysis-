# 📊 Retail Sales & Profitability Analysis

An end-to-end data analytics project focused on understanding sales
performance, profitability, product trends, and regional business
performance using SQL and Power BI.

## 🔍 Project Overview

This project analyzes retail sales data to identify what drives revenue
and profit across products, regions, customer segments, and time. SQL is
used to answer key business questions, while Power BI presents the
findings through an interactive dashboard.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

-   Evaluate overall sales and profit performance.
-   Track yearly sales and profit trends.
-   Identify high-performing categories and loss-making products.
-   Compare sales and profitability across regions.
-   Explore the relationship between discounts and profit.
-   Understand profit contribution by customer segment.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

-   **SQL (MySQL):** Data analysis, aggregations, grouping, filtering,
    and business queries.
-   **Power BI:** Interactive dashboard and business performance
    reporting.
-   **CSV:** Dataset source.

## 📁 Dataset

-   **Records:** 51,290 rows
-   **Columns:** 26
-   **Date range:** 2011--2014
-   **Key fields:** Order Date, Order ID, Sales, Profit, Category,
    Sub-Category, Product Name, Region, Segment, and Discount.

## 📈 Analysis Highlights

The SQL analysis explores:

1.  **Overall Business Performance** -- Total sales, total profit, and
    unique orders.
2.  **Sales & Profit Trends** -- Year-wise sales and profit, and the
    highest-sales year.
3.  **Product Analysis** -- Category-wise sales and profit, plus the top
    5 loss-making products.
4.  **Regional Analysis** -- Region-wise sales and profit,
    highest-profit region, and regions with negative profit.
5.  **Discount & Customer Analysis** -- Profit by discount level and
    customer segment.

## 📊 Power BI Dashboard

The Power BI dashboard presents key retail performance metrics and helps
users explore:

-   Sales and profit performance
-   Yearly business trends
-   Category and product performance
-   Regional profitability
-   Customer segment contribution
-   Discount-related profit patterns

## 💡 Project Outcome

The project demonstrates how SQL and Power BI can be used together to
convert raw retail data into meaningful business insights and support
data-driven decision-making.
